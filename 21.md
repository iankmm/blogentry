Title: Retrospect 21
Category: thoughts

Today, December 14th, 2025, marks my 21st birthday.

It’s often seen as the age when you’re finally trusted with responsibilities and freedoms that were once out of reach. While that idea is a bit symbolic, it does make me pause and reflect.

Looking back at the person I was when I wrote my blog at 18, I can see just how much I’ve grown. Of course, that growth comes with its share of regrets and things I would approach differently now but I think that’s part of the process.

At this point, I feel more certain about where I’m headed. My goals are clearer, and I’m steadily working toward them.

Here’s to 21 :)
