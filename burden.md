Date: 12-2-2025
Title: Evanesce
Category: thoughts
---
I have always believed that a lie and its consequence are born at the exact same moment. They are twins that cannot be separated no matter how much distance one tries to put between them.

I usually don't say a word and I don't rush to pull the curtain back when I spot one. I often find myself wondering why I’m so passive about it but the answer is always the same as exposure rarely benefits me. There is no prize for being the one to point out the crack in the foundation. Pointing it out just leaves you standing in the rubble.

Hence, I watch. I see the lie for what it is, a beautifully wrapped package. Some people are masters at the wrapping; they use expensive paper and perfect bows, making the fabrication look so much better than the boring, jagged truth. But I know what’s inside the box. I know that the prettier the packaging, the uglier the thing waiting underneath.

I stay silent because I know the math is absolute. You cannot have the lie without the debt.

It might not happen today, and it might not happen tomorrow. The liar might walk around for years, thinking they have gotten away with it, thinking they have outsmarted the system. But the consequence is always there, trailing behind them like a shadow. It doesn't matter if I expose them or not, because the lie itself will do the haunting. It will sit in the back of their mind, waiting for the perfect moment.

I don't need to act. I just need to wait. The consequence is already on its way.
